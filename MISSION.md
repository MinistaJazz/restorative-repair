# Why This Skill Exists

This skill is a resource. It is for anyone learning to train AI, and particularly for Black women entering the field  -  so that the architecture, the lineage, the practice, the citations, and the working examples are already here, in plain sight, when you arrive.

You do not have to build the foundation alone. The foundation is here. Take it. Use it. Make it yours.

## What this skill does not need from you

It does not need your permission to be useful to you. It does not need you to be credentialed or trained or vetted. It does not need you to ask first. It is here. It is open. Take what you need.

The only thing this skill asks of you, in return, is that you honor the source the way the lineage honors itself: name who came before, name who shaped the work, name who held the door open. That is the covenant. That is the only one.

## The Tubman line, in full

> *"I go to prepare a place for you."*
>  -  Harriet Tubman, reported last words, echoing John 14:2

The line is short on purpose. It is operational, not ornamental.

The line is reported through bedside testimony and secondary accounts, not through audio recording. It echoes John 14:2 and belongs to the scriptural language Tubman carried. She used the same promise when she left for freedom intending to return for her family, and it was remembered again at her death.

Harriet Tubman did not just escape. She returned. She returned with maps, with provisions, with timing, with the architecture of an underground network already in place by the time anyone she came back for needed it. The freedom she carried was infrastructure she was preparing, in advance, for the people she had not yet brought home.

This skill is that move at a different scale, in a different century, by a different woman, for a different kind of crossing. The infrastructure is here. The place is prepared.
